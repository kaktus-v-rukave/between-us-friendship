01 / The small things

Less grand gestures.
More ordinary moments.

A friendship is built in the things that are easy to miss. A few of them matter more than they seem.

01

Feeling seen.

Remembering their big day. Noticing when they go quiet. Asking a question and actually listening to the answer.

02

Room to be yourself.

Different opinions, different interests, different plans. You can care about someone without becoming the same person.

03

Showing up.

Sharing the fun is easy. Keeping a promise, making time, or saying “I was wrong” takes effort. That effort counts.

03 / A few words to keep

Good words.
Even better in practice.

A small friendship vocabulary.
Tap a word to look a little closer.

Trust+
noun / verb

Believing that someone will be honest and keep their word.I trust you to keep this between us.

Empathy+
noun

Trying to understand how another person feels.I can see why that was difficult for you.

Boundaries+
plural noun

Limits that show what you are comfortable with.I need some time alone tonight.

Reciprocity+
noun

Giving and receiving care, support, or effort. Both people take part.We make time for each other.
